Sharing and service providers

  • Lenders: we share your data with a bank or NBFC only after you agree to apply with that lender.
  • Service providers who process data on our instructions: Google (Sheets, Gmail and Apps Script, for the forms), Hostinger (website hosting), Google Fonts and Google Maps (they receive your IP address when pages load, and the map loads only when you choose to open it), and WhatsApp (only when you choose to message us). Some of these may store data on servers outside India, as the DPDP Act permits except for countries the Government restricts.

Security and breaches

We use reasonable safeguards: access to the enquiry sheet and mailbox is limited to the firm, with strong passwords and two-step verification. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected persons and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Rules require, with what happened, its likely impact and the steps taken.

Your rights

  • Access (section 11): a summary of your data we hold, how we use it, and with whom it was shared.
  • Correction and erasure (section 12): correct, complete or update your data, or have it erased.

Your duties (section 15): give accurate information, do not impersonate anyone, and do not file false complaints.

Children

Our services and forms are for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data of children; if a form is filled for a child, we need verifiable consent of a parent or guardian.
