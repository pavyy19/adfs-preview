01 Our Story

Local roots. State-wide reach.

AD Financial Services began in Mangalwedha with a simple idea: people deserve honest, realistic guidance before they take on debt. Too often, borrowers are pushed into the first product offered, at a rate and tenure they do not fully understand.

Today we work with leading banks and NBFCs to arrange every kind of retail and business loan throughout Maharashtra. Whether you are seeking a personal, business or other type of loan, our team listens carefully to your requirement and builds the solution around your financial goals.
